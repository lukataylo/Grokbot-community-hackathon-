afterglow

Grok Bot Hackathon · London

Every event photo, shared with consent.

afterglow turns a pile of camera rolls into a coordinated community launch, with no manual tagging, no lost photos and no posting into the void.

  1. 1

    Scan & consent

    Scan the event QR, sign in with X, add LinkedIn and choose whether you can be photographed or tagged.

  2. 2

    Upload

    Everyone drops their photos into one shared event gallery.

  3. 3

    Grok tags

    Grok finds the people who opted in and sends each photo to the people in it. Anyone who opted out is never tagged.

  4. 4

    Coordinated reach

    Grok schedules who posts, who comments and who reposts whom across X and LinkedIn, with copy ready to go.

  5. 5

    Leaderboard

    Points for photos, posts, comments and reposts. Top contributors win community merch.
